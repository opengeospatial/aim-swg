== AIM Agri Profile

=== Overview

The AIM Agri Profile (`https://w3id.org/demeter/agri`) is Tier 4 of AIM. It is the master profile of the model: it defines no classes or properties of its own and imports all agricultural domain modules together with the alignment between AIM and NGSI-LD (see <<annex_alignments>>).
